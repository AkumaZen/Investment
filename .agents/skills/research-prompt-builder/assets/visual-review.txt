## 8B. Visual quality review

Locked. Run this after section 8. Every section 8 check stays in force.

Answer each question for the whole report, and fix every NO before the final reply:
1. Does each chart answer a meaningful research question?
2. Is the chart type right for the data?
3. Are all figures consistent with the source data?
4. Are historical and projected figures easy to tell apart?
5. Are units, periods and legends clear?
6. Are titles insight-led?
7. Is management's explanation placed next to the financial data it explains?
8. Are tables readable on desktop and on mobile?
9. Are axes and scales honest?
10. Can every source be traced?
11. Is the report consistent with the locked design system in section 7?
12. Does every visual improve understanding rather than add length?
13. Does the report follow one declared reference family, with physical source-page precedents and justified departures documented?
14. Can each material thesis be traced from primary evidence through an operating driver to earnings, cash, capital needs and a falsification signal?
15. Were the reference patterns applied to actual page construction with readable typography, source notes and no unexplained geometry copied from text rectangles?

Final review, done twice:
- As an equity research analyst: is anything important missing, unsupported or misleading? Would a fund manager's first question be answered?
- As a report designer: is the hierarchy clear, is the key finding visible first, and is there any visual that repeats data shown elsewhere?

Put the findings of both reviews in the final reply, with what was fixed.

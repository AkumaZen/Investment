{{S1_TRACKABLES: the 6 to 10 trackables and dated triggers for this company}}

**S2. The business, simply.**

### Part B: management

**S10. Management analysis.**
- Who they are: founders, KMP and board; background and tenure from filings; succession and key-person risk; board independence.
- Skin in the game: holding trend, who sold and at what price, preferential or warrant participation, pledges checked in primary filings, pay vs profit, ESOPs.
- Capital allocation: every rupee raised and where it went vs the stated plan; acquisitions and the returns on them; unrelated ventures; where the cash sits today; dividends and buybacks.
- Do they deliver? Links to S11, with a credibility score as % of promises met. An annual-report-only company gets a statements register instead (as in Kabra), and a biotech gets a timeline tracker (as in Suven).
- How they communicate: tone per call (BULLISH, CAUTIOUSLY OPTIMISTIC, NEUTRAL, CAUTIOUS or DEFENSIVE); guidance direction across calls; what they repeat and what they stop mentioning; how they answer hard analyst questions, verbatim with speakers; what they talk about that is not yet in the numbers.
- Fair to minorities? Related parties, promoter-linked deals, valuation reports, disclosure quality, auditor remarks, ratings, litigation.
- Hard red flags: qualified audit, going-concern language, rising pledge, deviation in use of proceeds, loans to promoter entities, active investigation, repeated guidance misses.
- Verdict: OK, WATCH or RED, with one line of reasoning. A RED caps the rating.
{{S10_SPECIFIC: the people, raises, deals and claims to check, by name}}

**S11. {{S11_TITLE: Management claims checked, or Statements register}}.** Claim, date, speaker, what happened, status, and credibility in one line.
{{S11_SPECIFIC}}

### Part C: the future

**S12. Guidance and outlook.**
- Current guidance table: metric, number or range, period, speaker, verbatim quote, source. Covers revenue, margin, PAT, volume, capex, order book, exports and new products wherever given.
- Next quarter: what management expects, in their words. If they gave no number, say so instead of inferring one.
- Direction across calls: first given, later changes, current; each marked raised, held or cut.
- What must be true: the growth and margin the remaining quarters need for guidance to hold.
- Conflicts: deck vs call. Analyst claims are never presented as guidance, and broker numbers stay out.
- No guidance: said plainly, with the statements register used instead.
- S11 checks past promises and S12 sets out what is promised now. Together they feed the management guidance row in section 5.
{{S12_SPECIFIC}}

**S13. Future growth and triggers.**
- Growth build by driver: each driver sized bottom-up for the next 3 years and capped by capacity and balance sheet. Derived, not quoted.
- Trigger table: catalyst, timing, what management said, ₹ impact, bias, Confirmed or Probable, source.
- Strength: STRONG only if it appears in 2 or more primary filings with numbers and a date; otherwise MODERATE or WEAK.
- All triggers listed, including what management talks about that is not yet in the numbers, sector or government moves, and anything the user raised. If an expected trigger is absent, say so.
- Near-term (next 2 quarters) kept separate from long-term (next 2 to 3 years).
- Trigger dates: results, commissioning, approvals, warrant deadlines, each with what would confirm or break it.
{{S13_SPECIFIC: the drivers and triggers to size, by name, and any expected trigger to test for absence}}

**S17. Financials and quality of earnings.** P&L, cash vs profit, working capital, debt, share count, and the sector metrics: {{SECTOR_METRICS}}. Verdict OK, WATCH or RED.

**S18. Governance and filings.** Material filings in date order, promoters, related parties, auditor, credit rating, and data conflicts.

### Part E: risks, value and verdict

**S19. Risks and anti-thesis.**
- Anti-thesis: the strongest case against owning this stock, argued as a sceptic would, with real numbers. Not a list of generic risks.
- Bull claim vs rebuttal: each main bull point, the best counter-evidence, and which side the evidence favours today.
- What if we are wrong? If the stock halves in 2 years, what most likely caused it?
- Risk cards, 6 to 8, ranked: specific and falsifiable, each with likely ₹ or per-share impact and a "signal that confirms it" with a threshold, such as debtor days above 150.
- Link to value: the bear case in section 5 is built from these risk cards, and the signals feed the dated watch list.
- What would change our mind: the evidence that would kill the anti-thesis, and the evidence that would kill the thesis.
{{S19_SPECIFIC: the anti-thesis to test, in one or two lines, and the risks expected}}

**Appendix. Sources.** Every source with its link; the Disclosed, Inferred and Not Disclosed list for key numbers.

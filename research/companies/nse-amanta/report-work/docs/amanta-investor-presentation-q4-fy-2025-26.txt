Strategic Highlights
Profitability increased by 42% in FY26 on YoY basis 
Sustained revenue trajectory:
Q4FY26 revenue stood at INR 77 cr, registering a 7% YoY growth, while FY26 revenue reached INR 288 cr,
marking a 5% YoY increase, reflecting consistent growth.
Operational discipline sustains EBITDA margins:
EBITDA for Q4FY26 came in at INR 17 cr with margins at 21%, while FY26 EBITDA increased 6.06% YoY to INR
63 cr, with margins improving YoY to 22%, highlighting effective cost control and operational efficiency.
Profitability strengthens on robust execution:
Net Profit for Q4FY26 rose to INR 5.5 cr, up 28% YoY, while FY26 PAT grew 42% YoY to INR 15 cr, underscoring
strong profitability momentum led by decreasing in finance cost.
Improving earnings quality reflected in margins:
Q4FY26 PAT margin stood at 7%, while FY26 PAT margin stood at 5% compared to 4% for FY25 driven by
operating leverage and margin normalization.

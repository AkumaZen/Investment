MANUFACTURING FACILITIES

Unit I: General and Penem

Two fully segregated blocks with dedicated man and material movement controls, housing multi-product sterile and Penem manufacturing lines.

Floor Layout

Ground Floor: Manufacturing and packaging areas, raw material and finished goods storage, WFI generation system, WFI storage and distribution

First Floor: Quality Assurance, Regulatory Affairs, Formulation and Development departments, HVAC system, compressed air and nitrogen generation systems

Manufacturing Lines and Equipment

Manufacturing Lines: Lyophilized and Liquid Injectables (vials and ampoules), Dry Powder Filling (vials), Pre-Filled Syringes, Large Volume Parenterals (Liquid Vials)

Equipment: Vial washing machines, Dry Heat Sterilizer autoclaves, Aseptic filling systems (dedicated per line), Sealing systems

MANUFACTURING FACILITIES

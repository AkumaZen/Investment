## Early synthesis

The central historical issue is not whether Amanta added SteriPort capacity: management and the Q2 FY27 investor presentation report Line 3 operational in September 2026. The issue is the gap between early commissioning expectations and delivery, followed by a very fast expected utilization ramp, and whether incremental volume is converted into margins while polymer prices, depreciation and finance costs rise. SVP/Inhalation is the more valuable but slower, registration-dependent leg: its expansion date moved from January 2027 to Q1 FY28, while advanced-market filings for inhalation had not yet begun as of the Q2 FY27 call.

Reported revenue trajectory did improve: FY26 revenue was ₹288 crore (Q4 call) versus FY25 ₹275 crore in the Q3 call's historical comparison (about 4.7% growth by arithmetic); Q1 FY27 was ₹69 crore, +5% YoY per Q1 deck; Q2 FY27 was ₹83.1 crore, +17% YoY per Q2 call/deck. At the February Q3 FY26 call, management hoped for ~₹400 crore in FY27 (~33% increase) and described 20% CAGR as a two-year ambition; this was not an FY26 growth forecast. By November Q2 FY27, the CEO stated a FY27 full-year expectation of ₹420–430 crore, after also describing that as an annualized run-rate for Q3/Q4. The Q2 call separately confirmed an analyst-raised ₹400–420 crore FY28 target including SVP, but SVP revenue within it was not quantified. The two-year 20%+ CAGR / 25%+ EBITDA-margin aim is forward-looking and not demonstrated by Q2's 21.6% quarterly EBITDA margin.

## Best Q&A takeaways (ranked for analytical value)

1. **LVP delay and ramp are explicit.** Q3: April commercialization expected; Q4: next month; Q2: ~5 months late, commissioned September and 55% at early run rate; full operation forecast Nov 20. Strongest delivery tracker. (Q3 transcript physical pp.6–7; Q4 pp.3–4; Q2 pp.7, 10.)
2. **Clarify the commissioning timing.** Q1 call says FDA plan approval was received and validation was expected by Aug 18, with commercial production targeted for the last week of August; Q2 call later says commissioned in the first week of September. Q1 was a target, not confirmation of an August actual. (Q1 transcript p.4; Q2 transcript p.3.)
3. **Capacity is 11.6cr stated, ~12cr rounded.** Arithmetic from 6.6 to 11.6 is +5.0cr (~76%); the company rounds the capacity increase to 75%. Earlier calls describe roughly 12cr. (Q2 presentation pp.5, 9; Q2 transcript p.3.)
4. **Q2 call reports ~55% early Line 3 utilization.** Management forecasts full operation by Nov 20; the Q2 deck confirms first-week September commissioning and installed capacity but does not report a Sep-to-date unit-sales figure. (Q2 deck p.5; Q2 call pp.7–8.)
5. **FY26 revenue grew ~4.7% by arithmetic, but no ~20% FY26 target is substantiated.** Q3 management was discussing FY27: ~₹400cr, ~33% growth, with a two-year 20% CAGR aspiration. (Q3 call physical pp.3–5, 16; Q4 call pp.3–5.)
6. **FY27 ₹420–430cr now implies a sharp H2.** Q1 ₹69cr +5%, Q2 ₹83.1cr +17%, H1 ₹151.9cr +12%; if the Q2 call full-year range is used, H2 needs ₹268–278cr arithmetically. Q2 describes the same figure as annualized Q3/Q4 run-rate and full-year FY27 expectation. (Q2 call physical pp.5, 10.)
7. **Annual line sales and FY contribution are distinct.** Management retains ~₹110cr annualized LVP capacity revenue at optimal utilization but only expects ₹60cr from the late-starting line in FY27. (Q3 call p.11; Q2 call p.5.)
8. **SVP timeline slipped and advanced-market benefit is later still.** SVP expansion moved from January 2027 to Q1 FY28; inhalation dossiers are planned only after commissioning and tech transfer. (Q3 call p.16; Q2 call pp.4, 13, 16.)
9. **Management admits the new SVP line is product-specific.** BFS inhalation shapes/tooling differ from conventional products; line capacity by itself is insufficient without product pipeline. (Q2 call pp.6–7.)
10. **Inhalation pipeline count is ambiguous.** Management says the inhalation portfolio has six products, two commercialized, and “additional six” to be developed; unclear whether that means six total or six additional beyond the portfolio. Advanced-market filings remain pending. (Q2 call p.6.)
11. **Ophthalmic strategy is asset-light commercially.** Management says it does not plan to build a specialized marketing front end; it plans to license ophthalmic products in India and export markets. This limits both go-to-market cost and direct value capture. (Q2 call pp.13–14.)
12. **No direct SteriPort export claim.** Management corrected the questioner: previous Canada/Australia business was diluents/WFI, not SteriPort; SteriPort remains India-centric in its current strategy. (Q2 call pp.10–11.)
13. **Cost constrains product adoption.** Management says SteriPort is costlier than legacy and other two-port packs, so it is selectively used in oncology, ICUs/intensive cardiac and pediatric/anesthesia settings. (Q2 call p.8.)
14. **Safety evidence is company-reported.** Management claims more than 35cr SteriPort bottles since 2016 with no fungal contamination complaints; no independent evidence or comparator supplied on the call. (Q2 call p.8.)
15. **Share-of-market estimate is management's rough framing.** Management estimates Indian IV fluids at 190–200cr units, SteriPort at ~6% share with ~12cr capacity, plus 2.5–3% BFS share; category boundaries are described as “amorphous.” (Q2 call p.7.)
16. **High capacity utilization has distinct definitions.** Companywide plant utilization 95–97%; new SteriPort Line 3 at ~55% during initial balancing; management forecasts full rate from Nov 20. These are not directly comparable percentages. (Q2 call pp.7–8.)
17. **SVP near-term revenue goal is a management target, not a filed guidance bridge.** Current ₹55–65cr line of business targeted to ₹100–120cr, dependent on product and market mix; no dated sales bridge. (Q2 call p.9.)
18. **Solar savings will be assessed over a full run period.** Management repeats ₹9cr annualized saving; Q2 call says generation started end-Q1 and Q2 EBITDA benefited, but finance cost includes solar loan interest. (Q2 call pp.3–5.)
19. **Polymer inflation was absorbed but pass-through incomplete.** Q2 margin held near 21.6% despite raw-material volatility and price corrections; management expects normalization in 3–6 months. (Q2 call pp.4, 7–8.)
20. **New-line depreciation is already being charged.** CFO says full depreciation on new capacity was provided in Q2, while utilization remains in ramp; future margin expansion must outrun depreciation and interest. (Q2 call p.4.)
21. **Distribution coverage is established, further reach is hospital-level.** Management cites 200–250 channel partners nationwide and says incremental effort is more hospital penetration in critical-care micro-markets, not simply more distributors. (Q2 call pp.8–9.)
22. **No immediate next SteriPort capex planned.** Management says 12cr SteriPort plus ~6cr legacy capacity is enough for hospital demand; future focus is SVP and product value growth. (Q2 call p.9.)
23. **Market expansion competes with UK business under constrained capacity.** Q1 call says they had to choose between UK and other export regions due to constrained SVP capacity; new capacity is intended to allow broader coverage. (Q1 call pp.15–16.)
24. **Margin target lacks a quantitative bridge.** Q2 call claims 25%+ EBITDA margin in two years; actual Q2 margin 21.6%, with polymer inflation, depreciation and interest moving in opposite directions. (Q2 call pp.3–5.)

## Evidence labels and follow-up questions

- **Management-reported fact:** line commissioned/solar generating, reported revenue/margins, product pipeline counts, historical market/customer assertions. These are statements in company calls/decks and have not been independently verified in this memo.
- **Management forecast/target:** commercialization dates, full utilization dates, annualized revenue, solar savings, FY27 revenue, SVP commissioning, growth and margin targets, regulatory opportunity.
- **Arithmetic:** FY26 growth ~4.7%; H2 revenue needed for ₹420–430cr; capacity increase 6.6 to 11.6 = 5.0cr / 75.8% (company rounds to 75%).
- **Open checks for the main report:** (a) verify the reported first-week-September LVP commissioning date; (b) verify actual Q3 FY27 line sales/utilization and whether ₹60cr FY27 contribution remains achievable; (c) track Q3/Q4 margin, polymer price recovery and realized solar saving; (d) obtain revised SVP build schedule and confirm Q1 FY28 commissioning; (e) distinguish product registrations, filings, tech transfer, launch and commercial sales by country; (f) keep companywide export mix separate from SVP-specific export mix; (g) obtain evidence for efficacy/safety claims and SteriPort market share rather than treating management assertions as independently established.

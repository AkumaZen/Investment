AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S1 Investment view

Turning capacity into earnings and cash

A credible operating step-up is underway, but the current price already demands execution.

Amanta makes sterile liquid medicines and the containers that deliver them. Its premium SteriPort IV-fluid platform supplies the volume engine. Small-volume parenterals (SVP), especially inhalation and ophthalmic products, offer a potentially better mix. A captive solar plant can reduce electricity cost. These are three different profit mechanisms, with different evidence and timing.

Capacity has arrived; earnings quality is the test

The new SteriPort line began production on 2 September 2026. Capacity rose from 6.6 crore to 11.6 crore bottles a year. Q2 FY27 revenue rose 17.3% to ₹83.13 crore. Yet operating EBITDA excluding other income was only 20.7% of sales. Finance cost and depreciation still consumed roughly two thirds of that operating profit.

Source: AS p.1; P2 p.5; R27 p.3. PDF locators are physical pages.

The stronger opportunity is also the slower one

The planned SVP expansion adds 10.8 crore units and targets Q1 FY28 commissioning. Specialized tooling, formulation transfer and market registrations must follow the right sequence. Management explicitly called its ₹16 crore first-year sales illustration speculative. We do not value an unapproved glass-injectables idea or assume advanced-market approvals.

Source: C2 p.5; C2 p.12; C2 p.14; C2 p.15. PDF locators are physical pages.

Debt ratios improved faster than cash generation

FY26 net worth increased with the ₹126 crore gross IPO. Gross borrowings excluding leases nevertheless rose to ₹234.42 crore at March 2026, before falling to ₹194.56 crore in September. H1 FY27 operating cash of ₹21.94 crore did not cover ₹37.60 crore capex, even before interest payments classified as financing cash flows.

Source: AR p.52; AR p.53; R27 p.4; R27 p.5; R27 p.6. PDF locators are physical pages.

What the price requires

Our conditional base illustration reaches ₹413.7 crore sales and 25% operating margin in FY28E. At 14x operating EBITDA, the value is ₹331 a share versus ₹202.15 today. The result is highly sensitive to both the multiple and cash conversion. The upside requires both earnings delivery and a sustained valuation multiple.

What this means for you. Follow sold output, contribution after input costs, and cash after interest and capex. Installed capacity alone is insufficient evidence for paying a premium.

AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S2 Business in plain language

A sterile manufacturer sells reliability with each dose

Amanta earns when qualified, usable sterile products are sold and collected, not when bottles are merely produced.

A parenteral is a medicine administered by injection or infusion. Large-volume parenterals (LVP) include IV fluids used for hydration and carrying medicines. Small-volume parenterals (SVP) include water for injection, small injections and other sterile packs. Ophthalmic means eye care. Respiratory solutions are delivered through a nebuliser. These terms describe the use or dosage form, not separate listed subsidiaries.

Why the container matters

The medicine and its delivery system are linked. SteriPort uses a two-port polypropylene bottle made through injection stretch-blow moulding (ISBM). Aseptic blow-fill-seal (BFS) forms, fills and seals a plastic container in an integrated process. The container affects compatibility, handling and the risk of contamination. Specialized tooling and validated processes can create barriers, but manufacturing automation alone does not prove superior clinical outcomes or permanent pricing power.

Source: P2 p.13; P2 p.16. PDF locators are physical pages.

Where the cash comes from

Domestic branded products use a distribution network. Exports and product partnering add other routes to market. Product sales generated ₹281.6 crore of FY26 revenue before services and other operating items. The economic unit is a released, sold dose or bottle at a realized net price. More litres, more units and higher-value formulations need separate interpretation.

Source: AR p.65. PDF locators are physical pages.

What this means for you. Ask which gate has been passed for each growth idea. A machine installation proves only one part of the route to cash.

The SVP opportunity depends on product transfer, not just speed

Inhalation and preservative-free eye care have credible technical requirements; commercial receipts remain stage-dependent.

Management explains that some export markets do not want paper labels on the container because of spore risk. Product names may need engraving, with variable data printed on a tab. The call refers to 20 pipeline products, roughly 12 SVP products, six inhalation products, two already commercialised and an additional six to be developed. AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S5 Customers and partners

A distribution network helps; hospital conversion still matters

Customer diversification reduces single-buyer exposure, while hospital adoption and collections determine the quality of growth.

Why a hospital might pay more

Management positions SteriPort for oncology, critical care, cardiac intensive care, anaesthesia and paediatrics. In these settings, handling and delivery-system reliability may matter more than the lowest acquisition cost. But hospital budgets still constrain substitution. Management itself says the product is costlier than legacy and other two-port alternatives. Wide distribution is necessary; it is not evidence that every hospital will pay the premium.

Source: C2 p.7; C2 p.8; C2 p.9. PDF locators are physical pages.

What we can and cannot say about named customers

The source set does not identify customer names or licensees with product-level contracts. Analyst affiliations and the call organizer are not customers. There is no disclosed binding order book supporting the Line 3 annual revenue potential. The annual report’s no-customer-above-10% statement is useful, but it does not establish low geographic concentration, short collection periods or guaranteed repeat orders.

Source: AR p.74; C2 p.5. PDF locators are physical pages.

AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S6 Moat and competition

The moat is process-and-access, with limits to pricing power

Sterile manufacturing and established routes to market create friction for entrants; they do not create an unconstrained monopoly.

Two attractive claims that need careful handling

Management reports more than 35 crore SteriPort bottles sold since 2016 without a fungal-contamination complaint. This is an attributed safety claim, not an independent comparative trial or audit. The deck’s statement of 50-60% domestic share has an unclear market denominator; the call’s rough 6% IV-fluid share is obtained from around 12 crore capacity versus a 190-200 crore-unit market. Capacity divided by a market estimate is not actual sold market share.

Source: P2 p.16; C2 p.7; C2 p.8. PDF locators are physical pages.

A business advantage must show up in economics

Operating EBITDA excluding other income was ₹59.67 crore in FY25 and ₹59.86 crore in FY26, despite revenue growth. High utilization and technical differentiation have not yet produced expanding operating profit. That does not disprove the platform’s value, but it limits the claim that capacity addition alone deserves a structural multiple premium.

Source: AR p.52. PDF locators are physical pages.

What this means for you. The defensible view is a qualified manufacturing and distribution franchise with execution risk. Sustained net price, cash returns and product retention would strengthen the moat evidence.

AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S7 Quantity versus quality

High utilisation can hide different economic realities

The old plant, the new line and newly qualified products must be measured separately.

The business mechanism is operating leverage: a greater number of sold, released units shares existing overhead. The constraint changes as the plant ramps. At first it may be engineering stability; later it may be qualified products, hospital demand, polymer cost or cash to carry stock. A single utilization percentage cannot capture all of these constraints.

What this means for you. Request shipped volume, average net realisation and line-specific contribution together. Production without profitable shipments can consume working capital.

AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S11 Historical delivery tracker

The expansion was delivered later than first envisaged

The delivery record supports technical progress, while commercial timing deserves a discount for uncertainty.

Why the historical record matters for valuation

Delay costs are more than a shifted revenue line. Capital remains tied up before sales; fixed costs, regulatory work and interest can continue. Inhalation adds a further dependency: equipment timing affects product transfer and filings. A single delayed project is not proof of chronic failure, but repeated date movement warrants explicit delay cases rather than immediate full-year steady-state revenue.

We do not produce a misleading “promises met” percentage. The milestones are interdependent and have different economic weights. Revenue language is also too ambiguous for a mechanical pass/fail score. The next proof is the amount of incremental sales and cash, not another revised commissioning date.

What this means for you. Use the original dates as a baseline. Preserve both delivered assets and missed timing when assessing management credibility.

AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S15 Reality versus optionality

Keep current earnings separate from future possibilities

The existing business earns today; unapproved markets and hypothetical projects must not be capitalized as accomplished facts.

A disconfirming detail: SVP sales fell in FY26

The audited product note shows SVP product sales of ₹47.29 crore in FY26 versus ₹53.39 crore in FY25, down 11.4% (calc.). LVP product sales rose 10.1% to ₹232.99 crore. That distinction is obscured by a simple companywide expansion story. Management’s later ₹55-65 crore SBU sales description may use a broader period or perimeter; it is not a direct like-for-like replacement for the audited product-sales note.

Source: AR p.65; C2 p.9. PDF locators are physical pages.

Why we avoid a sum-of-the-parts valuation

The company has one operating/legal reporting perimeter and no disclosed product-level capital employed, contribution or overhead allocation. A separate high multiple for the whole SVP “option” plus a multiple on company EBITDA would double-count current earnings. We use one EV/operating EBITDA valuation and test the incremental product mix within that model.

What this means for you. Optionality earns a larger weight as the first unproven gate is passed. A portfolio list is not a licence to add unsupported value.

AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S16 Causal proof and falsification

The causal chain shows where each thesis can break

A growth driver becomes valuable only after product, customer, margin and cash tests all work.

An integrated test is more useful than isolated bullish facts

A factory can run at a high rate and still earn disappointing returns if it makes lower-priced products, needs large inventories or carries expensive debt. A pipeline can be technically credible but commercially slow. Conversely, a modest revenue increase can improve PAT sharply if funding costs fall. The report therefore links every major thesis to a model input and an observable failure signal.

For the base model, the first uncertainties are incremental Line 3 sales and SVP launch timing. The next are operating margin and working capital. Valuation is deliberately last because none of the attractive product statements removes the need to pay a reasonable price for the eventual cash.

What this means for you. At each update, change the first unproven link to “verified” only with new evidence. Do not move it merely because the narrative became more confident.

AMANTA HEALTHCARE | S17 Results and earnings quality

Q2 improved; reported PAT growth needs an exceptional-item bridge

Revenue rose 17.3%, while operating EBITDA margin was nearly flat. The prior-year IPO charge exaggerates reported PAT growth.

Why the PAT story sounds different on the call

Reported Q2 PAT increased 302.6% and H1 PAT73.3% (calc.). The call says 26% and 12%, respectively. Adding back the prior-year ₹2.6288 crore exceptional IPO expense to prior-year PAT gives Q2 adjusted PAT₹3.8363 crore and H1₹7.3435 crore, producing 26.7% and 11.3% growth. That broadly explains the spoken comparison, but is a simple pre-tax add-back to PAT, with no explicit tax normalization. We use reported figures and show the adjustment rather than silently replacing them.

Source: R27 p.3; R27 p.7; C2 p.4; C2 p.5. PDF locators are physical pages.

What improved operationally

Q2 revenue rose 20.8% sequentially. Operating EBITDA increased 18.3% from Q1, so operating margin slipped from 21.13% to 20.68%. Compared with Q2 last year, it was nearly flat at 20.67%. Depreciation and finance cost rose; the new-line benefit has not yet produced a clear margin breakout. This is a volume acceleration, with earnings conversion still at an early stage.

What this means for you. Use both reported PAT and pre-exception profit. A lower comparison base is not evidence of a fourfold improvement in underlying operating economics.

Why a better debt ratio is not the same as lower net debt

Gross debt excluding leases rose ₹39.42 crore in FY26, while net worth more than doubled after the IPO. By September gross debt had declined ₹39.86 crore, but cash equivalents fell ₹79.34 crore, so cash-only net debt increased. Source: AR p.52; C4 p.10; R27 p.4. PDF locators are physical pages.

Capital productivity has room to recover

FY26 revenue/closing total assets was 0.52x versus 0.72x in FY25 (calc.). Revenue divided by closing PPE plus CWIP was 1.03x versus 1.28x. The asset base expanded before commercial revenue. Using operating EBIT and average equity plus debt/leases less cash gives a simple FY26 pre-tax return on capital of about 12%, broadly near Screener’s rounded 12% but different from the company’s 11.1% definition. These are definitions, not conflicting economic facts.

Value the operating ramp, then deduct its funding

# Amanta Healthcare FY2025–26 annual report analysis

**Cut-off:** 10 November 2026. **Source:** Amanta Healthcare Limited Annual Report 2025–26, standalone financial statements. Source PDF: `report-work/docs/amanta-annual-report-2025-26.pdf` (81 physical PDF pages). Citations below state physical PDF page and both printed pages shown on that PDF page. Amounts in ₹ lakh unless stated. **Fact** means reported/disclosed event or figure; **management claim** means company narrative or assertion; **estimate** means forecast or forward-looking amount/timing.

The statutory auditor reports that intended FY26 use was ₹8,000 lakh, so FY26 implementation lagged prospectus timing; unused cash was in fixed deposits / monitoring account. [Annual report PDF pp. 51, 80; printed pp. 98–99, 156–157]

The report says it relies on issuer-provided information and does not independently audit/verify it. [CRISIL MA Report, PDF pp. 3–10; especially p. 9]

## Business, products, operations

- **Fact:** The company manufactures sterile liquid parenterals at Hariyala/Kheda, Gujarat, with domestic branded sales, international sales, and contract manufacturing/product partnering. FY26 report describes four LVP lines (two conventional single-port and two SteriPort ISBM), and three SVP lines (two ABFS and one conventional 3-piece container). Reported installed capacities: LVP 5.7 crore units excluding SteriPort, SVP 20.9 crore units, SteriPort 6.6 crore bottles. [Annual report PDF pp. 9–10; printed pp. 14–17]
- **Fact:** Product range covers large-volume IV fluids, electrolytes and anti-infectives; small-volume sterile water/diluents, injections, ophthalmics, irrigation and respiratory/nebulization products. Pack/fill sizes range 2 ml to 1,000 ml; closure options include nipple head, two-port, twist-off, luer-lock and screw. [Annual report PDF p. 8; printed pp. 12–13]
- **Management claim:** SteriPort uses ISBM with random copolymer polypropylene and two-port administration; company claims container integrity, lower contamination, tensile strength and drug compatibility advantages, especially in oncology/critical care. It says SteriPort contributed ~42% of FY26 operating revenue. Treat these as issuer claims; the report offers no independent comparative clinical or product-level margin evidence. [Annual report PDF pp. 6, 12; printed pp. 8–9, 20–21]
- **Fact:** Export footprint is reported as 20 countries in FY26, 47 products registered across 120 international jurisdictions; FY26 geographic revenue was ₹11,069.63 lakh outside India and ₹17,349.76 lakh domestic (company’s geographic note reports revenue from operations of ₹28,419.39 lakh). No customer/group exceeded 10% of revenue. [Annual report PDF pp. 11, 74; printed pp. 18–19, 144–145]
- **Fact:** Domestic branded channel has ~320 distributors/stockists and ~96 sales/marketing staff; management describes exports into Africa, Latin America, UK and other markets, with product registrations across regimes. [Annual report PDF p. 11; printed pp. 18–19]
- **Management claim / moat:** Report characterizes sterile manufacturing as capital-, energy- and compliance-intensive with low tolerance for sterility failures, and cites three decades’ operating experience, WHO-GMP and ISO certifications as entry barriers. It does not quantify market share, relative cost, yields, customer retention, or switching costs. [Annual report PDF pp. 9–10; printed pp. 14–17]
- **Fact:** Report lists ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, WHO-GMP and Gujarat FDCA certification. EU MDR application confirmation (April 2024) is not equivalent to CE marking or marketing authorization. [Annual report PDF pp. 10, 13; printed pp. 16–17, 22–23]
- **Fact:** FY26 line utilization was reported as LVP 93%, SVP 98% and SteriPort 90%. The chart series for FY23–26 is LVP 97%, 93%, 91%, 93%; SVP 89%, 94%, 99%, 98%; SteriPort 73%, 83%, 91%, 90%. A nearby management statement says current utilization is 96%, without defining the aggregation method. [Annual report PDF p. 10; printed pp. 16–17]
- **Management estimates:** The FY26 letter targets SteriPort expansion from 6.6 crore to ~12 crore bottles/year; pipeline of ~20 products; expected growth in branded domestic and export markets. The FY26 capex graphic shows ₹130 crore FY26P and ₹25 crore FY27P, i.e. estimates in the graphic, not audited cash capex. [Annual report PDF pp. 6, 10; printed pp. 8–9, 16–17]

## Accounting, liabilities and key exposures

- **Fact:** Contingent litigation liabilities at 31 March 2026 total ₹1,698.78 lakh: GST ₹1,659.08 lakh (mostly input-tax-credit disputes), income tax ₹11.18 lakh and DPCO 1979 ₹28.52 lakh. Auditor identifies tax contingencies as a key audit matter. Amounts are claims/disputes, not recognized liabilities; outcome judgments remain uncertain. [Annual report PDF pp. 47, 67–68; printed pp. 90–91, 130–133]
- **Fact:** Under Advance License Scheme, incremental export obligation was US$41.40 lakh / ₹3,691.95 lakh; unprovided customs duty liability if unfulfilled was ₹713.92 lakh at FY26 end (vs ₹3.06 lakh year earlier). This is distinct from the litigation contingent-liability subtotal. [Annual report PDF p. 67; printed pp. 130–131]
- **Fact:** Capital commitments on PPE contracts not provided for: ₹1,880.26 lakh (vs ₹2,861 lakh prior year). [Annual report PDF p. 67; printed pp. 128–129]
- **Fact:** Auditor says no statutory auditor resignation during FY26; Price Waterhouse Chartered Accountants LLP audit opinion unmodified; internal controls opinion also unmodified. KAM was provisions/contingent liabilities. [Annual report PDF pp. 47–51; printed pp. 90–99]
- **Fact / audit evidence limitation:** Accounting audit-trail function operated throughout year; database-level edit-log not independently verified due to inability to obtain sufficient evidence. Auditor notes no tampering observed and management-preserved logs. [Annual report PDF pp. 48, 77; printed pp. 92–93, 148–149]
- **Fact:** Trade receivable gross balance ₹4,452.21 lakh; ₹4,433.44 lakh undisputed considered good and ₹18.77 lakh disputed considered good; no receivables classified as significantly increased credit risk or credit-impaired. - **Fact:** The company states no single customer or customer group accounts for ≥10% of revenue. This reduces disclosed customer concentration but says nothing about supplier concentration. [Annual report PDF p. 74; printed pp. 144–145]
- **Fact:** MSME supplier principal outstanding ₹343.19 lakh; interest due ₹7.10 lakh and further interest remaining due ₹0 in FY26. [Annual report PDF p. 68; printed pp. 132–133]
- **Fact:** Loan note describes a ₹1,875 lakh unsecured bank loan jointly availed by company and a director; company’s portion outstanding ₹1,551.34 lakh. Director’s residential property secured the joint loan and director separately bears his share, with repayments passed through the company. Certain borrowings have personal guarantees of the CMD/promoter. [Annual report PDF pp. 63, 71; printed pp. 122–123, 136–137]

## Material risks and diligence gaps from this report

1. **Execution / capital productivity:** IPO-funded expansion ran behind its FY26 milestone; as of Sep 2026, reported expenditure remained below full net proceeds. Track commissioning, SVP line completion, utilization, incremental volume and actual returns.
2. **Very high utilization:** Existing LVP/SVP lines are reported ~93%/98% utilized, so outages, maintenance, validation, staffing and ramp-up yield could constrain near-term shipments; validate actual production and capacity definition (units vs bottles).
3. **Quality/regulatory:** Sterile product recalls, contamination, data integrity or regulatory findings could have outsized consequences. FY26 includes audit-trail database-level verification limitation; seek remediation details and regulator inspection outcomes.
4. **Disputes and export obligations:** GST disputes are large (₹16.59 crore) and customs export obligations carry up to ₹7.14 crore unprovided duty risk. Obtain status, legal counsel view, compliance schedule and expiry/fulfilment evidence.
5. **Input-cost exposure:** CRISIL flags packing-material price volatility; no supplier concentration or cost pass-through detail is disclosed in the annual report.
6. **Related-party oversight:** Material land purchase from CMD and payment to a foundation where CMD is trustee require independent-approval, valuation and continuing-disclosure review.
7. **Forecast vs realized:** ₹9 crore annual solar savings and capex-driven margin expansion are management estimates; verify realized generation, tariff offset, borrowing cost, uptime and start dates.

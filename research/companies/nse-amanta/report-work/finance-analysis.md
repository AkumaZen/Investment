# Amanta Healthcare: financial inputs and reconciliation

## Source and accounting basis

Amanta Healthcare Limited reports one business segment and states in its FY26 annual report that it has no subsidiaries, joint ventures or associates. The analysis therefore uses the company’s standalone statements; there is no consolidated statement set to substitute. The FY26 annual report is audited. The September 2026 quarter and half-year results are unaudited and carry an unmodified limited review opinion.

The company’s audited annual statements and Q2 FY27 filings report rupees in lakhs. This note and `financial-data.json` convert those amounts to ₹ crore by dividing by 100. EPS is rupees per share; share counts are actual shares. FY23 and FY24 rely on Screener’s rounded annual series, while FY25 and FY26 are taken from the FY26 annual report wherever exact statement values are available. Quarterly data are from the standalone Screener series, which aligns with the official Sep-26 statement.

Sources: [FY26 Annual Report](https://www.amanta.co.in/public/assets/pdf/Investors/Annual-Report/amanta-annual-report-2025-26.pdf), printed pp. 98-102 (PDF pp. 52-54) for statements and equity; printed pp. 135-136 (PDF pp. 70-71) for related parties; [Q2 FY27 Board Outcome](https://www.amanta.co.in/public/assets/pdf/Investors/exchange-announcements/amanta-outcome-of-board-meeting-held-on-03112026.pdf), PDF pp. 3-6; [Screener AMANTA](https://www.screener.in/company/AMANTA/), standalone data fetched 9 Nov 2026; NSE official [9 Nov 2026 bhavcopy](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_09112026.csv).

## Cash flow and funding

FY26 CFO of ₹48.45 crore was near FY25’s ₹46.62 crore, but investing outflow expanded to ₹93.61 crore, chiefly ₹80.19 crore cash capital expenditure. Financing supplied ₹125.95 crore net, of which IPO proceeds were ₹126 crore gross before ₹16.65 crore issue costs; secured term-loan proceeds also funded capex/refinancing. FY26 ending cash rose to ₹81.01 crore from ₹0.22 crore, but included earmarked IPO money and a refinancing loan temporarily parked at year end.

H1 FY27 CFO was ₹21.94 crore, below the ₹27.65 crore in H1 FY26. Cash capex was ₹37.60 crore; cash and cash equivalents fell ₹79.34 crore to ₹1.67 crore. Financing cash flow was negative ₹52.92 crore, including ₹27.08 crore repayment of institutional term loans, ₹15.76 crore repayment of unsecured loans, and ₹6.19 crore interest paid on financial-institution loans. These figures show growth capex and debt service continue to absorb internal cash even as quarterly earnings improve.

The annual and H1 statements classify interest paid in financing cash flows. For an operating cash conversion measure after interest, subtract the reported interest cash payments from CFO before comparing with capex. Do not call CFO minus capex “cash available to equity” without this adjustment.

## Valuation-input limits

The report model can use the FY26 baseline, current issued shares and market close above. It should keep operating profit separate from other income, explicitly model interest, depreciation, taxes, capital expenditure and debt paydown, and avoid deriving enterprise value from debt plus leases without stating the lease treatment. H1 FY27 cash and debt should be used to calibrate funding pressure, not annualised mechanically. The expansion-capacity revenue figures are management opportunities/targets and require separate product qualification and sales evidence; they are not booked revenue or a forecast in these financial inputs.

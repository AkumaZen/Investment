MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
“Q2FY27 is a strong validation of the strategic choices we made earlier. Our transition from an API 
manufacturer to a focused FDF manufacturer, and the scale-up of operations on that platform, is now clearly 
visible in our performance. Operating income grew over 50% year-on-year, with operating EBITDA margin 
expanding to 18.02% and PAT margin to 13.21%, reflecting the strength of our business model.
The scale-up of our operations is delivering operating leverage, while the shift in business mix is lifting 
margins. Raw material cost has come down as a share of revenue, and our growth is coming from core 
operations rather than other income. Sequentially, revenue and operating EBITDA both grew by over 25%, 
building on a strong Q1. This momentum carried through the first half, with H1FY27 operating revenue of 
₹420.97 crore, up 35.79%, while operating EBITDA grew more than 4x.
With a net-debt-free balance sheet, substantial cash and investments, and our ₹250 crore capex programme 
funded through internal accruals, we are well placed to scale. Supported by deeper own-brand penetration in 
key export markets and the ongoing Samba EU-GMP upgrade, we continue to expect FY27 revenue growth of 
more than 50%, a medium-term revenue CAGR of 20-25% and EBITDA margin expansion of 200-300 BPS. We 
remain focused on building a compliance-led, differentiated FDF platform and sustaining profitable growth.”
N.R. Munjal
Chairman and Whole Time Director

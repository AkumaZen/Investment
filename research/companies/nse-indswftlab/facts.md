## Identity

- Ind-Swift Laboratories Ltd, NSE INDSWFTLAB, BSE 532305; Chandigarh-region group; company secretary Pardeep Verma [deck p1].
- Incorporated 1995 as an API and CRAMS maker with 481+ DMFs across 33 molecules [deck p5].
- API and CRAMS business sold by slump sale to Synthimed Labs (a portfolio company of India Resurgence Fund, a Bain and Piramal platform) for Rs 1,650 cr; Ind-Swift kept a 7.80% stake in Synthimed [deck p6].
- Proceeds repaid all external debt [deck p6]; Screener borrowings fell from Rs 862 cr (FY23) to Rs 17 cr (FY24) [Screener].
- Ind-Swift Ltd (the group's finished-dose company) merged into Ind-Swift Laboratories, effective 31 Mar 2024 by NCLT order [deck p7].
- Today: a finished-dose formulation (FDF) business, 82.7% export and 17.3% domestic in Q2 FY27 [call p1].

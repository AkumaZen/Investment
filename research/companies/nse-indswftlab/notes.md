## Headline answer to the core debate (feeds S1 and the final reply)

The H1 FY27 step-up is real in the P&L but is not yet a proven business.
About Rs 80-90 cr of the Rs 110.95 cr H1 revenue increase came from new customers [call p11, E], and one partner molecule (Ezetimibe+Atorvastatin) went from Rs 25 cr in Q1 to Rs 58 cr in Q2 [call p5].
That is a launch-and-partner ramp, not yet a repeat-order engine: the first unproven link is repeat orders turning into cash, because receivables rose Rs 66.26 cr and inventory Rs 65.52 cr in a year [deck p30, E] while operating cash flow was negative for three years [Screener].
Rs 1,500 cr by FY30 needs only 16.3% a year after FY27 if FY27 lands at Rs 954 cr [E], so the long target is less demanding than the near-term H2 need of Rs 532.87 cr (+63.5% on H2 FY26) [E].
Of the Rs 3,035 cr market value, Rs 348 cr is net cash and up to Rs 624 cr is a management-valued unlisted stake carried at Rs 32 cr [deck p30, AR26 p80, E].
Verdict for step [3]: a business in the making on top of cash and a stake; the evidence that would make it a durable business (Q3 revenue at least Rs 266 cr, receivable days below 90, positive H2 operating cash flow) arrives in Feb and May 2027.

## Anti-thesis (written before the bull case)

The strongest case against owning Ind-Swift at Rs 349:

1. Profit has not become cash. Operating cash flow was negative for three years, Rs -260 cr in total [Screener], and in the last year inventory plus receivables grew Rs 131.8 cr, more than twice H1 PAT of Rs 55.47 cr [deck p29, p30, E]. A business whose profits sit in receivables is not yet worth a peer multiple.
2. The growth is narrow. One partner molecule is Rs 83 cr of H1 revenue [call p5], two molecules were 26% of FY26 revenue [deck p19, E], and 72-81% of the H1 increase came from new customers [call p11, E]. In contract manufacturing the partner controls volume and price ("it is VITRIS only who is driving the sales" [call p16]), and European generic prices fall each year.
3. The guidance keeps moving. Seven of 22 claims changed and two capital-use claims missed in ten months (claims ledger); CDMO FY27 was halved from Rs 200-220 cr to Rs 100-130 cr between Aug and Nov 2026 [D-Q1 p15, deck p23]; the call misstated growth by 35 points [call p2]. H2 must be +63.5% on H2 FY26 [E] while management says "we don't foresee any risk" [call p16].
4. A third of the value is not the business. Rs 348 cr of net cash (of which Rs 16 cr is pledged) and a stake management values at Rs 546-702 cr but carries at Rs 32 cr [AR26 p80], with no sale date, no tax disclosure, and a PE owner who decides timing.
5. The cash is not obviously the minorities'. About Rs 308 cr sits in promoter-linked or unlisted instruments, a Rs 62.89 cr loan has no repayment terms, the company owns shares of the promoter entity that funds it, two promoter directors got 72% and 100% raises, and the audit fee is Rs 4 lakh [AR26 p64, p68, p80-p81, p93].
6. More dilution is coming even with net cash: Rs 137.20 cr of new promoter warrants in Sep 2026 [CARE-Q2 p4] for a capex plan described as funded "from internal accruals" [deck p3]; the reason is not explained.
7. The price already assumes delivery. At Rs 349 the core trades at 11.4x FY27 guidance EBITDA; if FY27 is the bear Rs 891 cr at 17.5%, EBITDA is about Rs 156 cr and the core is 13.2x a number that has not been shown in cash [E].

If the stock halves in two years, the most likely cause is H2 FY27 at the Q2 run rate, a fourth year of negative OCF, and a Synthimed sale that does not happen, all three together (Inferred).

## Bull case (after the anti-thesis)

- The margin moved from 5.47% to 17.97% in one half year on revenue up 39.5% [deck p29]; operating leverage on a flat employee base (Rs 37.36 cr in Q2 vs Rs 38.95 cr in Q1 [deck p28]) is real.
- Site approvals (MHRA, TGA, Health Canada) and 540+ own registrations take years to replicate [deck p7, p8].
- Viatris is a 10-year relationship [tr-q1 p12] and gave Ind-Swift exclusive worldwide supply of two products [deck p14].
- Net cash and the stake mean the downside is partly covered by assets, and the core is priced at 11.4x guidance EBITDA against a 17.9x peer median [E].
- Promoter is buying at Rs 196 and has converted 14 lakh within three weeks [NSE-A, NSE-C].

Which side the evidence favours today: the anti-thesis on cash and governance, the bull case on margin and approvals; the H2 FY27 numbers decide it.

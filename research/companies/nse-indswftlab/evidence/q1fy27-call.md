## Opening remarks

- CDMO partnerships with Viatris, Manx (UK) and Arrotex (Australia) commercialised in the quarter, "expected to contribute an incremental revenue of Rs 200 to Rs 220 crore in FY27" [tr-q1 p3].
- Dossiers filed 2,100+, up from 1,915+ [tr-q1 p4].
- Ezetimibe + Atorvastatin "saw the sharpest growth"; Esomeprazole with Viatris and Arrotex expected to launch in FY28 [tr-q1 p4].
- Mix: own-brand exports 57.20% of sales (48% in Q1 FY26); contract manufacturing in exports 26.64%; branded generics 6.41% (10%); ethical 6.14% (7%); domestic contract manufacturing 3.61% [tr-q1 p4].
- Operating revenue Rs 186.08 cr vs Rs 153.58 cr; operating EBITDA Rs 33.32 cr vs Rs 8.66 cr; margin 17.91% vs 5.33%, +1,258 bp [tr-q1 p4].
  Our check: 33.32 / 8.66 = 3.85x, so the stated "2.85x" understates the jump [E]. 33.32 / 186.08 = 17.91%, PASS.
- PAT excluding exceptional item Rs 24.68 cr vs Rs 8.12 cr ("2.04x"); PAT margin 13.26% vs 4.99% [tr-q1 p4].
  Our check: 24.68 / 8.12 = 3.04x, so "2.04x" is also off by one turn [E].

## Internal and cross-call conflicts

1. FY27 revenue: Rs 900 cr expected [p13] vs ">50% growth" on Rs 635.89 cr (= Rs 954 cr [E]) in the deck; Q2 call accepted Rs 1,000 cr as a "minimum internal target" [tr-q2 p6].
2. CDMO incremental FY27: Rs 200-220 cr [p3] vs Rs 100-130 cr in FY27 and Rs 200-220 cr over 2 years [tr-q2 p1].
3. CDMO share of exports: 45% of Rs 750 cr [p8] vs a 35/65 contract/own target [tr-q2 p5].
4. Dossier addition: 400+ [p11] vs 330+ registrations in FY27 [tr-q2 p1] (dossiers and registrations are different counts; the two are not the same metric).
5. Capex period: 2.5 years [p6] vs two years [p14].
6. Multiples stated as 2.85x and 2.04x where the reported figures give 3.85x and 3.04x.

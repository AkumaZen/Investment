| Synthimed value | Rs 8,000-9,000 cr; "FY26E EBITDA Rs 600 Cr" [D-Q3 p6] | Same page | Rs 8,000-9,000 cr (USD 1 bn) [D-Q2 p6]; CFO said Rs 7,000-8,000 cr [tr-q2 p15] |

Reading:
- The FY27 margin story was raised (600-800 bp) in Aug and cut back (200-300 bp) in Nov, while the H1 margin actually rose about 1,250 bp. The 200-300 bp in the Nov deck sits on top of a higher base; the deck does not say which base [Inferred].
- The CDMO FY27 number was halved between Aug and Nov (Rs 200-220 cr in FY27 to Rs 100-130 cr in FY27), and the Rs 200-220 cr was pushed to a two-year window. That is a cut in near-term CDMO, offset by a higher overall revenue target (FY30 Rs 1,500 cr).
- Synthimed's FY26E EBITDA was Rs 600 cr in the Q3 FY26 deck and its FY27 budget is "approx. Rs 750-odd crores" [tr-q1 p6]. Rs 8,000-9,000 cr is about 10.7-12.0x that FY27 budget [E].

## Business and stake

- "retains a 7.80% equity stake in Synthimed Labs"; Synthimed "currently valued at Rs 8,000-9,000 Crores" [AR26 p10].
- Standalone balance sheet does not show a Synthimed equity line. It shows 32,00,000 0.001% compulsorily convertible debentures (CCDs) in Synthimed Labs at Rs 32.00 cr, measured at fair value through profit or loss (FVTPL), unchanged from FY25 [AR26 p80].
  Reading: the 7.80% stake appears to be held through these CCDs [Inferred]. A Rs 32 cr carrying value against a company-stated Rs 624-702 cr look-through value means the stake is almost entirely off the book; under FVTPL it should be re-measured each year, and no re-measurement gain is booked [Inferred].
- 18% Synthimed NCDs of Rs 48.00 cr redeemed on 10 Dec 2025 [AR26 p81].
- FY26 standalone revenue split: domestic Rs 128.24 cr (FY25 Rs 150.41 cr), export Rs 484.39 cr (FY25 Rs 399.23 cr), total Rs 612.64 cr [AR26 p93]. Domestic fell 14.7% [E].
- No segment reporting beyond geography: "other segmental information is not ascertainable" [AR26 p93].

## Promoter warrants

- Chairman's letter: in Feb 2026 Essix Biosciences "converted fully convertible warrants into 51 Lakhs equity shares at Rs 121 per share, investing approximately Rs 61.71 Crores", promoter holding from about 39.5% to 43% [AR26 p11].
- Directors' report: "80,00,000 equity shares were allotted to M/s. Essix Biosciences Limited ... upon conversion"; all Essix warrants converted [AR26 p40].
- Conflict: 51 lakh vs 80 lakh shares. Rs 61.71 cr / Rs 121 = 51.0 lakh shares [E], which fits the 75% balance paid on 51 lakh. 80 lakh x Rs 121 = Rs 96.8 cr. Most likely 80 lakh in total were converted during FY26 with 51 lakh in Feb 2026 [Inferred]; to be reconciled with BSE allotment filings.
- Scheme: 45,15,495 shares allotted to promoter-group holders of the erstwhile Ind-Swift Ltd [AR26 p40].

## 1. Why it matters and the core debate

Ind-Swift Labs sold its API business to Synthimed Labs for Rs 1,650 cr, kept a 7.80% stake, repaid all debt, and merged in the group's finished-dose company Ind-Swift Ltd from 31 Mar 2024 [deck p6, p7].
What is left is an export-led finished-dose (FDF) maker whose operating EBITDA margin went from 5.47% in H1 FY26 to 17.97% in H1 FY27 on revenue up 39.5% [deck p29].
Management guides FY27 revenue growth above 50%, Rs 1,500+ cr by FY30 and a 20% margin [deck p3, p23, call p4, p7].
Core debate: is this a durable export FDF business (own dossiers plus multi-year CMO contracts) on a path to Rs 1,500 cr, or a launch bump from a few partner molecules (Atorvastatin+Ezetimibe, Viatris Ibuprofen and Clarithromycin) sitting on top of a cash-and-stake holding company whose operating cash flow has been negative for three years [Screener]?

## 2. How one unit earns money

Revenue = registrations x launched SKUs x volume x price, in 85+ countries [deck p7].
Own brands: Ind-Swift owns the dossier and registration and sells through distributors and its own reps; export own-brand gross margin 50-55% [deck p23].
Contract manufacturing (CMO / CDMO): makes products on the partner's licence at an agreed transfer price; the partner drives volume [call p16].
Domestic: branded generics, ethical (gynaecology and paediatric, 70-80% gross margin) and P2P third-party manufacturing on advance payment [deck p11, p23].
Q2 FY27 split: export 82.7%, domestic 17.3% [call p1]; own brands 55.75%, CMO 26.99%, branded generics 6.84%, ethical 5.43%, P2P 5.00% [deck p20].

## 9. Connecting the dots

Revenue line rose 39.5% in H1 FY27 to Rs 416.35 cr [deck p29], while inventory rose 63.7% to Rs 168.35 cr and receivables 39.3% to Rs 234.79 cr [deck p30].
Inventory plus receivables grew Rs 131.8 cr in a year, more than twice H1 PAT of Rs 55.5 cr, and cash fell Rs 65.5 cr to Rs 322.81 cr [deck p29, p30, E].
Net working capital is Rs 269.4 cr, about 118 days of annualised H1 revenue, against the CFO's Rs 350 cr at Rs 1,500 cr (about 85 days) [call p13, E].
So the margin step-up is real in the P&L but not yet in cash; if working capital days do not fall toward 85, Rs 1,500 cr needs about Rs 485 cr of working capital at today's days [E], and the Rs 250 cr capex plus that build could absorb most of the Rs 348 cr net cash.

## Objects, funding needs and delivery plans

All ₹572.764cr net proceeds scheduled for **estimated FY27** deployment (pp130-131). No proof of actual spending is supplied by a prospectus written before allotment. CARE Ratings appointed monitoring agency; Audit Committee and CARE to review quarterly until full utilisation; pending proceeds only scheduled commercial bank deposits, no lien and no equity-market investment; no bridge loans proposed for repayment; objects not externally appraised (pp103,142). Actual monitoring reports and bank balances must replace intent in a later utilisation conclusion.

Working capital funds supplier advance margins, doré/scrap procurement and bullion inventory (pp132-136). Customer pays ₹0.30m per kg gold or per 30kg silver advance; company says it pays suppliers 100% order value, funds blocked roughly half day to two days before final customer receipt/delivery (p135). Business needs capital despite short turnover, since rolling customer receipts remain committed. Company says commercial bank/NBFC restrictions prevent financing gold stock-in-trade for enterprise sales; manufacturing/industrial-processing exception is separately acknowledged (pp34,134). Internal accruals, intercorporate funding and equity are therefore important; do not interpret low bank debt as absence of funding dependency.

Standalone net WC ₹463.192cr FY26 vs ₹130.102cr FY25; FY26 inventory ₹267.680cr, receivables ₹123.012cr (pp136-137). Management projections FY27 net WC ₹1,366.405cr, FY28 ₹1,939.601cr are **estimates**, not realised assets/revenues (p137). FY25 low year-end inventory/receivables/payables explained by nonworking days/holidays; year-end ratios need average-balance context (p138).

Twenty spot centres at March 2026 (nine direct, eleven franchise), across 13 states. Plan to add **15 centres by FY29**, primarily Tier3/4; no completed-addition assertion follows (p243). These centres need inventory/logistics/liquidity; the separate 106 Finkurve Gold For All centres are described as touchpoints without physical inventory (p244). Do not combine different centre categories or call the entire bullion group asset light.

## Business and operating evidence

Two verticals: enterprise/international, and consumer products through Gold For All. SPOT is a proprietary OTC delivery bullion platform, not a regulated securities exchange or subscription software business; valid GST business buyers, delivery normally within two working days (pp233-235,246). No brokerage or terminal fees: margin embedded in quoted metal prices (p241). Also sells bullion to AMCs for ETF units and trades gold/silver ETF units (pp234,246). Lab-grown diamond trading facilitated in FY26; no separately quantified profit or GMV substantiates materiality (pp238,244,246).

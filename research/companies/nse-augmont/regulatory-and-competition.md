# Augmont: regulation, custody and competition

Analysis cutoff and source-access date: **7 November 2026**. Security scope: **Augmont Enterprises Limited, NSE AUGMONT / BSE 544888, India, INR**. This is a supplementary evidence note, not legal advice or a valuation. The uploaded Q2 FY27 call extraction in `facts.md` is management evidence; external primary evidence below is kept distinct. Undated live webpages have not been independently archived at their historical version.

## Digital gold and EGR are separate products

SEBI's **8 November 2025**, PR 70/2025, *Caution to public regarding dealing in ‘Digital Gold’*, is the latest directly applicable SEBI public warning located in this review. It says digital gold is neither notified securities nor regulated commodity derivatives, operates outside SEBI's remit, and entails counterparty/operational risk. Securities-market investor protections therefore do not apply. In contrast, SEBI identifies exchange commodity derivatives, mutual-fund gold ETFs and exchange-traded EGRs as regulated gold products. Exact short excerpt: **“They operate entirely outside the purview of SEBI.”** This warning is not itself a prohibition on every digital-gold sale, nor an approval of a particular provider. A newer enacted digital-gold framework was not verified; press reports of discussions cannot substitute for final rules. [SEBI, published 8 Nov 2025; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/nov-2025/1762580259409.pdf).

NSE's *Electronic Gold Receipts* product page, marked updated **23 June 2026**, identifies EGRs as SCRA securities, with SEBI, exchanges, clearing corporations, depositories and vault managers in the ecosystem. NSE provides trading; NSE Clearing settles cash/EGR transfers; depositories maintain demat balances; vault managers handle physical deposits, storage and withdrawals. Settlement is T+1 and margins include VaR, ELM and MTM. The page lists both 995 and 999 purity products. An EGR is thus a different legal and operational claim from an app ledger for purchased gold. [NSE, accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/about-electronic-gold-receipts). The linked foundational regulatory source is SEBI's *Master Circular for Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs)*, **24 June 2024**; the landing page and title were verified, but detailed annexures were not fully re-reviewed here. [SEBI master circular, accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/jun-2024/master-circular-for-electronic-gold-receipts-egrs-_84369.html).

**Investment implication (judgment):** migration from app gold to EGR/ETF can compete with digital-gold holdings even while generating conversion, refining or liquidity opportunities for Augmont. Regulatory status of an EGR activity does not confer SEBI product protection on all other Augmont offerings.

## NSE relationship: verified role and limits

NSE's **26 September 2026** release, *NSE empanels Augmont as key partner for promotion of Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR)*, names **Augmont Gold Tech Private Limited, subsidiary of Augmont Enterprises Limited**, as the empanelled partner. Scope includes simplifying creation/extinguishment, promoting trading, and supplying technical and physical support for conversion between gold and EGRs. Exact excerpt: **“key partner for the promotion of Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR)”**. This establishes exchange empanelment, not by itself a SEBI vault-manager registration, exclusive nationwide franchise, guaranteed volume or disclosed fee entitlement. The release has no registration number authorising Augmont as vault manager, broker or depository. [NSE official release, published 26 Sep 2026; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com//web/pressrelease/2026-09/PR_cc_26092026_20260926125056.pdf).

**Post-call update:** the NSE homepage displays actual EGR activity for **6 November 2026**, with aggregate turnover of **₹33.15 crore** and displayed volume **2.28 lakh**. These are exchange-segment statistics, not Augmont revenue or profit. This supports operating activity by the cutoff rather than treating all EGR trading as a future project. A formal primary launch notice proving that this was the *first* live trading day was not located; do not conflate May segment-framework launch, September empanelment and November trading activity. [NSE market homepage, snapshot dated 6 Nov 2026; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.nseindia.com/).

Still required: actual role-specific SEBI/member registrations, executed commercial agreement, exclusivity/termination terms, accepted refinery brands, named vault operator, unit creation/extinguishment charges, liquidity obligations and Augmont-specific volumes.

## Custody and metal ownership: contractual promises versus independent proof

Augmont's undated consumer **Terms & Conditions** name **Augmont Goldtech Private Limited (formerly Augmont Precious Metals Private Limited)** as platform operator. They say accepted purchases trigger a corresponding physical-bullion purchase and delivery to a custodian; Augmont bears bullion insurance cost. Customers can sell back or request physical delivery; displayed buy/sell spreads vary. Storage is free for five years subject to company amendments; a current maximum storage period of ten years is stated. Jewellery redemption attracts the partner jeweller's terms/quality responsibility. Specific product terms can override general terms. These are contractual representations, not independent reconciliation or an insurance certificate. [Augmont Terms & Conditions, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://augmont.com/terms-conditions).

The separate **FAQ** claims customer gold is held in segregated vaults, outside Augmont's balance sheet, managed through an independent trustee, insured and audited, with customer ownership surviving a shutdown. The same page acknowledges that digital gold has no current SEBI/RBI product framework. These statements should be reported as **company assertions**, especially bankruptcy protection: no executed trust deed or insolvency opinion was examined. Its marketing description is **“LBMA-compliant”**, which does not demonstrate Good Delivery accreditation. [Augmont FAQs, undated, page says latest update three days earlier without a calendar date; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://augmont.com/help/faqs).

**Evidence gap:** trustee and custodian legal names, trust deed, whether bars are allocated or pooled, customer beneficial ownership mechanics, bar list, reconciliation frequency/results, audit signatory, current metal-liability coverage, insurance insurer/limits/exclusions/loss payee, transit cover, and enforcement waterfall were not independently verified. A legacy trustee-certificate URL failed through both web and Exa retrieval; no reliance is placed on third-party assertions that IDBI Trusteeship is the current trustee. Do not turn “fully insured” into protection from price losses, fraud, all operational loss or every insolvency outcome. Gold leasing, if elected, requires separate analysis of title transfer, lessee, collateral and default risk rather than assuming ordinary vaulted-gold protections.

## Lending: Finkurve lends; Goldtech provides platform support

Augmont's **Gold Loan** page explicitly identifies **Finkurve Financial Services Limited** as loan provider and prints RBI licence **13.00316**. This is a company-disclosed licence number, not an independent RBI-register revalidation. [Augmont Gold Loan, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://augmont.com/gold-loan).

**Arvog's official site identifies Arvog as Finkurve Financial Services Limited** and describes Goldtech providing the Gold For All platform to facilitate its gold-loan business. Arvog is therefore a lender brand; Gold For All is a platform/product brand. Common promoter association does not collapse these into Augmont Enterprises' own lending book. Do not import Finkurve's AUM, gearing, NIM, NPAs, bank facilities or earnings into Augmont consolidated figures without an actual consolidation basis. [Arvog, *Home*, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.arvog.com/).

Augmont's general terms also describe the app's role as technology support for FFSL's offline gold-loan service for existing users. **Accounting question:** obtain the signed services agreement and current related-party disclosure to identify fixed fees, transaction fees, brand fees and any guarantees/first-loss exposure; loan collateral custody and borrower complaints follow lender-specific documents. [Augmont Terms & Conditions, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://augmont.com/terms-conditions).

## Refinery accreditation

BIS's official downloadable *List of Refineries/Mint granted Licence* includes **Augmont Enterprises Pvt. Ltd., Rudrapur**, licence **8390002519**, marked operative but with displayed validity **6 March 2022**. This is historical evidence of a licence, **not proof of validity on 7 November 2026**. It cannot establish the current Navi Mumbai plant's licence. [BIS list, publication date not supplied, URL directory Nov 2022; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Refineries-latest-list.pdf).

BIS's refinery guidance distinguishes domestic licensing scope (gold bullion/coin, 995/999 fineness, design and weight) and laboratory prerequisites. A BIS licence is not synonymous with LBMA Good Delivery status. [BIS, *Refineries*, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.bis.gov.in/refineries/?lang=en). LBMA maintains its own Good Delivery list. This review did not independently substantiate an Augmont listing; the machine-readable list did not expose complete entries consistently. Therefore label Augmont LBMA accreditation **unverified / aspirational per management call**, not awarded. A verified current certificate and dated listing announcement are required before upgrading that status. [LBMA, *Good Delivery Current List*, accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.lbma.org.uk/good-delivery/gold-current-list).

## Bank finance: a real constraint, but avoid an absolute conclusion

The **30 September 2025 DRHP**, printed p.47 and p.125, discloses that enterprise sales cannot obtain debt finance from the identified regulated lender classes under the company's interpretation of RBI gold-lending restrictions. It nevertheless says working capital was historically funded with **internal accruals and inter-corporate borrowings**. This supports an enterprise funding constraint, not a claim that every Augmont entity has no debt or that all borrowing is illegal. Working capital funds advance margins, bullion/doré procurement and refining; the claim should be reconciled to the final prospectus and current balance sheet. [Augmont Enterprises DRHP filed on NSE, published 30 Sep 2025; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/Registration_30092025232853_AELDRHP.pdf).

RBI's **6 June 2025** *Lending Against Gold and Silver Collateral Directions*, version updated **29 September 2025**, explains restrictions on primary-gold lending. Historic **19 November 2012** bank-finance instructions expressly preserved genuine jeweller working-capital finance and the Gold Metal Loan scheme. Thus “banks never finance gold businesses” is too broad. [RBI 2025 Directions, accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12859); [RBI, *Bank finance for purchase of gold*, 19 Nov 2012; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=7695&Mode=0).

**Current-law gap:** RBI's Commercial Banks–Credit Facilities Directions landing page now identifies an **1 October 2026** update; full operative annexure and application to Augmont's mixed trading/refining activities were not successfully extracted. Do not claim the older version settles current eligibility, particularly any manufacturer/industrial-input exception. Obtain final applicable directions, end-use eligibility and a company-specific legal/lender explanation. [RBI current directions landing page, accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13156).

## Competition by economic model

| Model / example | Relevant competition or substitution | Comparison discipline |
|---|---|---|
| Principal bullion trading/refining: Augmont SPOT; established bullion suppliers and refiners | Procurement terms, assay trust, delivery reach, spreads and working-capital turns | Benchmark gross profit per gram and cash capital employed; gold-value turnover is not platform fees. SPOT operator's own FAQ describes replacing telephone bullion ordering. [Augmont SPOT FAQ, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://spot.augmont.com/faq) |
| Integrated refiner and digital-gold seller: MMTC-PAMP | Physical bars/coins, trusted purity, digital distribution and buyback | Official digital-gold page offers insured vault storage, physical redemption and sale-back, naming Universal Trusteeship Services as trustee. Its own accreditation claims require checking against LBMA, not extrapolation to Augmont. [MMTC-PAMP Digital Gold, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.mmtcpamp.com/digital-gold) |
| Digital-gold supplier/custody arrangement: SafeGold / Digital Gold India | Partner APIs, consumer acquisition, vaulting and redemption | SafeGold's terms document vault keeper, security trustee and insurance, explicitly noting uninsured events can put customer gold at risk. Similar product functions limit any assumption that trustee-backed gold alone is a unique moat. [SafeGold Terms of Use, undated; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.safegold.com/assets/terms-and-conditions.pdf) |
| Regulated EGR infrastructure and gold ETFs | Alternative holding/trading route for financial gold exposure | Product substitutes rather than operating-company valuation peers; regulatory protection, demat requirements, liquidity, charges and conversion mechanics differ. [SEBI warning, 8 Nov 2025](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/nov-2025/1762580259409.pdf) |
| Lenders, including Finkurve/Arvog | Borrowing against existing jewellery; lender owns underwriting and credit exposure | Compare Augmont service economics with service businesses; do not apply lender NIM/AUM multiples to Augmont bullion revenues. [Arvog official site, accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.arvog.com/) |
| Jewellery retailers / partner jewellers | End-consumer jewellery purchase and exchange | Retail design, making charges, store productivity and brand spending differ from bullion spreads. They can be customers/partners and substitutes simultaneously; consumer jewellery outsourcing does not settle manufacturing activity at every subsidiary. |

**Judgment:** integration can improve sourcing/distribution convenience, but no sourced evidence here establishes exclusive customer access, durable premium pricing or switching costs sufficient to remove commodity and funding competition. No forced valuation comparable set is proposed.

## Regulatory search false-positive control

SEBI's **June 2026 interim order in Rajesh Exports**, WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC6/32431/2026-27, names **Rajesh Exports Limited and Rajesh Mehta** as noticees. “Augmont Enterprises” appears as a customer name in the comparison of conflicting sales submissions **made by REL**. This is not evidence that Augmont is a respondent, that Augmont financial statements were misstated or that SEBI made a misconduct finding against Augmont. Independent reconciliation of that counterparty history remains possible diligence; attribution of REL findings to Augmont would be improper. [SEBI official interim order, June 2026; accessed 7 Nov 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2026/ORDER_1780476153.pdf).

## Profile

- 30+ fermentation APIs, 150+ DMFs, 180+ approved formulation products, 12 approved ANDAs (7 products marketed in the US).
- 194 R&D staff, 22+ non-infringing processes, 10+ pipeline products.
- Fermentation 1,250 m3: Unit I Dholka 450 m3 (FY2000), Unit III Limbasi 800 m3 (FY2021).
- Unit II formulations, Valthera (FY2016): 869 million OSD units.
- Unit IV injectables, Valthera (FY2025): 13 million liquid vials, 12 million dry powder vials, 2,200 kg sterile lyophilised.
- R&D spend: FY22 Rs 26 cr (3.6%), FY23 30 (3.5%), FY24 23 (2.2%), FY25 27 (2.3%), FY26 29 (2.8%).
- Top-10 customer share: FY22 47.7%, FY23 44.3%, FY24 42.0%, FY25 37.8%, FY26 37.6%; 70+ countries, 300+ customers.
- Inspected by USFDA, EU GMP, WHO, PMDA; US subsidiary Stellon Biotech.
- Therapy areas: immunosuppressants (Tacrolimus, MMF, MPS, Cyclosporine, Sirolimus, Pimecrolimus, Everolimus premix, Voclosporin); oncology (Temsirolimus, Everolimus, Romidepsin, Mitomycin, Dactinomycin, Midostaurin); antibacterial (Mupirocin, Polymyxin B, Teicoplanin, Vancomycin, Fidaxomicin); antifungal (Anidulafungin, Caspofungin, Micafungin, Amphotericin B, Nystatin); others (Lovastatin, Pravastatin).

## Call, Q2 FY27 (Ankur Vaid, JMD and CEO; Raviraj Karia, CFO)

- ANDA approvals: Tofacitinib, MMF oral suspension. US DMF filed for Fidaxomicin. Unit II USFDA inspection closed with one minor procedural observation. ANVISA approval for Unit III.
- Inspira dermatology division launched; India TAM about Rs 5,000 cr; Rs 400-500 cr revenue needs at least 4-5 years.
- Formulation weak: emerging-market customers switching to buying API; US business moving from Advergent to Stellon. Domestic formulation +29%.
- Gross margin down about 400 bps on solvents, power and fuel, mix; called temporary.
- [G] FY27 growth "slightly better than historical 18-19%"; API to build on 18-19%, with injectables, CDMO and formulations as upside.
- [G] EBITDA margin 38-40% over the next few years. Tax rate 24-25%.
- [G] Capex: maintenance Rs 20-30 cr a year plus about Rs 50-60 cr each in FY27 and FY28.
- [G] Injectables: TAM about Rs 5,000 cr; about Rs 300 cr revenue over 3-4 years; contracts expected in H2.
- CDMO: one commercial project, about $2 million a year, exclusive supplier for an NCE.
- Utilisation: Unit I 75%, Unit III 55%, Unit II 21%, Unit IV 5%.
- Cash and equivalents about Rs 445 cr; zero debt.
- Analyst pushback (Kotak): two-year sales flat, three-year CAGR about 5.5-6%; management calls the FY26 decline "global, not structural".
- Immunosuppressant market grows about 10%; Concord's growth comes from second-source wins.
- Nystatin qualified with US customers on validation quantities, not yet commercial. Fusidic acid launching. Romidepsin up on customer US launches. Oncology nearly doubled.
- API moves to the formulation units at market price, which hurts gross margin while those units run at low utilisation.
- Stellon contributions expected in H2.

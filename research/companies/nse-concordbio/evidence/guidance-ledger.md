Quantified items add up to about Rs 40-45 cr against a revenue fall of Rs 145 cr (1,200 to 1,055).

The Q4 FY25 quarter (430, +35%) was not described as one-off in the Q4 FY25 call; FY26 later attributes lumpy bulk buying to the prior pattern (Q4 FY26 p4), which suggests FY25 Q4 held some front-loaded orders [judgment].

Pattern: operating facts (tax, mix, product launches, plant commissioning eventually) are delivered; growth and margin targets and new-business timing slip, and the targets are later re-labelled rather than withdrawn.
This supports an AMBER management-delivery verdict: honest about facts and reasons, unreliable on timing and on the size of the medium-term targets [judgment].

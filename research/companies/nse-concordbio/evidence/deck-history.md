## 3. The Q4 FY25 spike, located

| Quarter | API | Formulation | Domestic | Export | Total | Source |

| Q4 FY23 | 233.6 | 39.0 | 132.4 | 140.2 | 272.6 | deck-q4fy24 p6 |

| Q4 FY26 | 263.8 | 62.3 | 168.7 | 157.4 | 326.1 | deck-q4fy26 p8-p9 |

Q4 FY25 revenue of Rs 429.9 cr was 35% above Q4 FY24, and the jump was concentrated in domestic sales (+50%) and API (+37%).
Q4 FY26 fell back to almost exactly the Q4 FY24 level (Rs 326.1 cr vs Rs 319.0 cr).
Reading [E]: the FY26 "decline" is mostly the unwinding of one unusually large quarter sold largely to Indian customers; the decks and the Q4 FY25 call do not call it a one-off, so this stays a judgment.
Data conflict: Q4 FY24 domestic/export is 159.7/159.3 in deck-q4fy24 p6 and 159.5/159.5 in deck-q4fy25 p8 (restated); totals agree.

Utilisation in FY23 per deck-q1fy24 p9 and p24 (sourced there to the prospectus): Unit I about 75%, Unit II about 10%, Unit III about 32%.
Utilisation at Q2 FY27 per the call (p18): Unit I 75%, Unit III 55%, Unit II 21%, Unit IV 5%.
So Unit III moved from about 32% to 55% in three and a half years while the company ran an approximately flat FY24-FY26 top line; the remaining fermentation headroom is about 360 m3 of the 800 m3 unit [E].

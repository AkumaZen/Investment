## 0A. Why we are covering this business

- What it is: Alembic is a Vadodara drug maker that sells generic medicines in the US, branded medicines to Indian doctors, partner-led generics in about 100 other countries, and bulk drug ingredients (API) to other makers. US generics and API are export businesses that live on new approvals; India is a steady prescription business.
- Why it caught our attention: Q2 FY27 revenue rose 36% to Rs 2,603 cr and EBITDA margin jumped to 22.2% from 17% [Q2 FY27 call p5], against FY22 to FY26 sales growth of 8.5% a year and an operating margin stuck at 12% to 16% [Screener].
- Why now: management raised FY27 growth guidance twice in six months, from "low double digit" (15 May 2026) to about mid-teens (4 Aug 2026) to about 20% to 25% (4 Nov 2026), while the bosutinib 180-day exclusivity that helped drive the jump ends in late November 2026 [Q4 FY26 call; Q1 FY27 call p11; Q2 FY27 call p5, p6].
- The core debate: once bosutinib exclusivity fades, does Alembic keep a base that grows about 20% with an EBITDA margin near 20%, or does it fall back to 10% growth and a 16% margin?
- What the market seems to believe: at Rs 1,017 the stock trades at 23.7x TTM EPS of Rs 42.92, which already includes the exclusivity quarter, and at 29.6x FY26 EPS of Rs 34.33 [Screener, E]. The price assumes most of the step-up holds.
- What would make us care, and what would make us drop it: we care if Q4 FY27 US revenue (the first full quarter after exclusivity) stays above Rs 800 cr and EBITDA margin stays at 18% or more. We drop it if US revenue falls below Rs 700 cr and margin drops back to 16% or lower.
- What the reader will be able to decide: how much of the H1 FY27 jump is a durable base and how much is a one-off, and whether Rs 1,017 pays for the one-off.

## Global incumbent anchors (FY2025)

- WuXi AppTec reported WuXi Chemistry revenue of RMB 36.47bn (+25.5% YoY) and small-molecule D&M revenue of RMB 19.92bn (+11.4% YoY). Its small-molecule D&M pipeline had 3,452 molecules at year-end: 83 commercial, 91 Phase III, 377 Phase II, and 2,901 Phase I/pre-clinical. It added 839 molecules and reported 310 R-to-D conversions during the year. [WuXi AppTec FY2025 results, Chemistry / Small molecule D&M sections] These counts indicate scale and progression, but do not equal contracted revenue or Indian outsourcing opportunity.
- WuXi AppTec reported >4,000 kL small-molecule API reactor volume and RMB 11.37bn TIDES revenue (+96%); TIDES combines peptides and oligonucleotides. The company said its TIDES backlog grew 20.2%, customers 25% and molecules 45% YoY. [WuXi AppTec FY2025 results, TIDES section]
- WuXi Biologics reported US$23.7bn total backlog at 31 Dec 2025: US$11.5bn service backlog plus US$12.2bn potential milestone fees. US$4.5bn fell within three years. Company annual report cautions milestone timing and realization probability depend on project success/progress. Treat total backlog as partly conditional and the three-year figure as a subset. [WuXi Biologics FY2025 annual report p.8 / FY2025 results, Backlog section]

## Prompt instructions implied by evidence

1. Separate research/CRO, innovator development and commercial manufacturing, formulation CDMO, generic/API, and non-pharma CRAMS revenue. Require issuer segment evidence before assigning CDMO exposure.
2. Test molecule-stage progression, commercial launches, backlog composition/timing, customer concentration, site qualification, modality capability, capacity utilization and realized segment revenue.
3. Compare Indian issuer evidence with global incumbents as context only; do not infer Indian share gain from global demand metrics or treat company pipeline/backlog as revenue.
4. Reconcile restructuring, listed parent/subsidiary and acquired-business perimeters before compiling historical growth or cohort totals.
5. This discovery list is not exhaustive; use current exchange and primary issuer sources to discover omitted listed entities and confirm every classification.

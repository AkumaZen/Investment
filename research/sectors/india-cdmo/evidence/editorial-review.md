# Editorial review: CDMO sector prompt

**Reviewed:** `prompts/draft.md`, `prompts/slots.json`, and `evidence/universe.md` as found on 10 Nov 2026. Review only; shared prompt files were not edited. The universe explicitly says it is a discovery register, not exhaustive, and some candidate identities/exposures remain unresolved.

## Assessment

The draft follows the requested sector-specific, company-comparable analysis well: it separates economic cohorts, traces sponsor demand through qualification to cash, requires an expanding universe and boundary register, keeps detailed valuation last, and makes unknown disclosure visible. The AX reference plan in `slots.json` is tailored to sector mechanisms, comparison, risk, company entries, capacity/process and valuation; all six required columns are present. Image, navigation, whitespace and render-QA instructions are already carried in the draft's design/build section. No research claim about the CDMO cycle is presented as an established fact; prompt-stage hypotheses are explicitly conditional.

## Concrete fixes / gates for the builder

1. **Make universe completeness executable, not implied.** The roster in `universe.md` contains verified, mixed, candidate, adjacent, unresolved and private names; it expressly is not exhaustive. Keep the current no-cutoff/expand rule, but require an execution-date register row for *every* candidate with identity, listing status, pharma-CDMO evidence, cohort, include/exclude/hold decision, source and unresolved evidence. Reconcile exchange/company discovery results before describing coverage as complete. The draft mostly does this already; do not turn the present candidate roster into a closed list or label all names core CDMOs.
2. **Add stable evidence/claim identifiers and definition bridges.** Existing bracketed citations, source manifest and Disclosed/Inferred/Not Disclosed labels are useful, but generic tags such as `[Screener]` do not identify a specific source record. Carry stable source IDs and claim/calculation/assumption IDs through the report and dataset. Add checks for KPI/segment definition changes, reclassifications, recurring “one-off” adjustments and restatements; recompute under prior definitions where possible. This is the main useful addition from the external market-research and reporting-quality skills.
3. **Review the fixed rating cutoffs.** S1 currently says BUY at ≥20% upside, HOLD within 20%, SELL at ≥20% downside, and a RED management verdict caps at HOLD. Treat these as an explicit house-policy choice sourced to the installed valuation skill or justify/test them as analyst conventions. They are not evidence-based universal thresholds; avoid allowing a categorical management cap to obscure the value range and rationale. If retained, show the underlying scenario/value range and sensitivity before the rating.
4. **Keep execution model routing aligned with standing instructions.** The Luna low/medium split matches the workspace research instruction. The additional instruction “if GPT-6 Luna is unavailable, stop” may block otherwise independent work; consider requiring a logged dependency and continuing only work independent of Luna, then state exactly which deliverables are incomplete. Do not silently substitute another model. This is especially relevant because the prompt also says to run to completion and continue independent research when a source is unavailable.
5. **Design handoff gate:** ensure the final assembly resolves `{{INCLUDE:7}}` with the staged absolute paths for the AX manual, JSON, PDF, guide and portable reader, and carries the AX typography recommendation/measured-vs-recommended distinction. `slots.json` records profile and page IDs but does not itself declare typography. Keep recommended readable type sizes; do not reproduce AX's dense 8.04 pt company-entry body. Section 6 already covers official issuer images, clickable PDF contents/bookmarks, page density, reproducible build and every-page visual/link inspection.
6. **Small copy fix:** S17 ends `Do not label unavailable metrics as zero.. Verdict OK, WATCH or RED.` Remove one period and clarify whether OK/WATCH/RED applies to the earnings-quality scorecard only or is a separate company verdict. Avoid collapsing distinct accounting, management and investment conclusions.

## Coverage and section coherence

- Company-by-company treatment is explicit in S20 and the issuer atlas, and the boundary register preserves excluded/unlisted/adjacent companies. Maintain separate cohorts for integrated innovator CRDMOs, formulation CDMOs, mixed groups, and adjacent/API candidates.
- The initial questions name key issuers and sector contrasts, while the execution universe is intentionally broader. Keep the question list illustrative and ensure it does not displace the roster-driven coverage requirement.
- Five-year history, latest eight comparable quarters and all available historical guidance are consistent: use the shorter display windows but preserve older source history for promise tracking and cycle interpretation.
- Demand, global peers, capacity and financial conclusions are framed with evidence requirements and caveats. Continue separating market size, qualified serviceable opportunity and issuer revenue; no global CAGR should be mechanically allocated to Indian firms.
- Output requests are ambitious but not internally incompatible: full PDF/HTML plus dataset/model/source/QA artifacts are named, there is no fixed page cap, and valuation/verdict remain last. Confirm that the builder's reference insertion and later task deliverables stay consistent with this output contract.

# CDMO sector report design handoff

## Selected profile

**REPORT_PROFILE: AX (Axis sector comparison).** CDMO investing is organized around sector-wide mechanisms—outsourced development/manufacturing demand, customer qualification, molecule/program conversion, facility utilization, process capability, and capital intensity—then compared across several companies. AX is designed to connect common sector mechanisms to multiple company entries. Reuse its comparative architecture and company-entry pattern, while replacing generic building-material macro pages with CDMO-specific evidence and avoiding AX's crowded 8.04 pt entry prose.

## Typography and visual system

**Measured source evidence (AX):** interior sheet 595.44 × 841.68 pt; ArialMT narrative, typically 9 pt in sector pages; company-entry page 32 uses 8.04 pt narrative; headings commonly 11.04–12 pt; table/chart labels often 8.04 pt; source notes commonly 6.48–6.96 pt. Axis magenta accents #AB2862 / #951A4E; secondary neutral fills include #D9D9D9 and #FCEBE0. JSON text regions are measured text boxes, not semantic layout frames.

**Recommended reconstruction (not measured source tokens):** A4 portrait; Arial if available, otherwise name and verify a metrically close sans substitute; 10 pt narrative / 13 pt leading, 13–14 pt major headings, 8.5–9 pt tables, 7.5–8 pt source notes. Use a restrained single accent plus neutral fills on a white field. Keep a narrower narrative measure with full-width exhibits where useful, and a right facts rail only for compact company-level facts. Reflow or split pages to preserve readability instead of copying AX's 8.04 pt company-page density or small notes. Do not copy Axis branding, logos, recommendation stamps, or house disclosures.

## Working taxonomy and decision rule

- **Innovator CRDMO/CDMO/CRO:** outsourced discovery, process/formulation development, clinical supplies and/or commercial manufacturing for innovator biotech/pharma. Distinguish discovery/research revenue from regulated commercial manufacturing, and verify molecule stage, customer concentration, repeat work, backlog/pipeline conversion and capacity.
- **Formulation CDMO/contract manufacturing:** manufacture/develop finished dosage forms for third parties. This is not interchangeable with innovator molecule lifecycle CRDMO; verify own-brand revenue and contract share.
- **API supplier / generic pharma:** APIs, intermediates or own generic formulations may include custom synthesis or external manufacturing, but do not presume material CDMO revenue. Require issuer segment disclosure/customer evidence.
- **CRO:** research/services without development/manufacturing breadth; separate from CDMO and CRDMO.
- **Adjacent chemical CRAMS:** custom synthesis/contract manufacturing in agrochemicals, specialty chemicals or fluorochemicals is adjacent evidence, not pharma CDMO; segregate pharma revenue and exclude from core until verified.

## Six discovery findings for prompt design

1. The investable core is not one homogeneous CDMO set: at minimum separate integrated innovator CRDMOs, custom synthesis/API businesses, sterile injectable or formulation contract manufacturers, and CRO/service-only work.
2. The strongest directly evidenced integrated CRDMO cohort in this pass is Syngene, Sai Life and Anthem. Official issuer evidence specifically supports discovery-to-manufacturing breadth; compare segment revenue mix and molecule progression rather than applying a single CDMO multiple.
3. Several large listed names (Piramal Pharma, Jubilant Pharmova, Laurus, Neuland) combine CDMO with other material businesses. Segment-level reporting and allocated capex/capital returns are essential.
4. Cohance is the relevant current listed identity for the old Suven Pharmaceuticals exposure in Screener. The prompt must trace scheme/restructuring and avoid stitching old standalone history into current consolidated numbers without reconciliation.
5. Formulation contract manufacturers (Akums, Innova Captab, Windlas) serve a different outsourcing market from innovator CRDMOs; their margin, customer, regulatory and asset economics should be analyzed as a separate peer bucket.
6. The broad “CRAMS” label is a false inclusion risk: Hikal, PI Industries, Anupam Rasayan and Navin Fluorine require a pharma-specific revenue/segment screen. Aragen did not resolve as a listed stock and belongs only in competitor context unless listing status is separately evidenced.
